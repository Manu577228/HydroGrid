# Competition traceability matrix

Requirements come from the supplied submission brief. A status of **Verified** means the cited evidence supports that requirement on the indicated release or branch. **Participant** and **Publication** mean a human attestation or permission is still required. [Decision IDs](MODEL_DECISION_LOG.md) give the recorded configuration.
